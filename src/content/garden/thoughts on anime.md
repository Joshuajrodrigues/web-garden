Like a lot of people around the world I was introduced to anime thanks to Dragon Ball Z. It would back then only premiere at 10pm. I would have to bite and claw and scratch in order to make an excuse from my parents to stay up just a few more seconds, so that I may get to catch the episodes.

I don't think my parents or anyones back then truly understood what anime was, sure it was "cartoons" but there was quite a significant diffrence between Looney toons and Naruto.

I think a better comparison to anime would be indian drama serials in terms of style, but much higher quality and standard.
