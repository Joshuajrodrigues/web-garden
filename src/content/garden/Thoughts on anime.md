I belive humans are extremely creative. And the testament to that creativity is right now in this day and age is anime.

A combination of art music and story telling amime often relies on the same mediums as "cartoons". Where it deviates is in terms of story and the variations in art styles.

Which also happen to be the two things that I admire most about this medium and keep coming back to it. Its what captured my attention when I was a wee little kid in grade 5 to now.
