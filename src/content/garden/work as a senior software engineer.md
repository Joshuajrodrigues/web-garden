I graduated college in Aug 2021 and while everyone else were preparing to take vacation or find jobs I already had one (YAY ! ?). Which was a bit weird as my degree hadn't even reached my doorstep yet.

(Degrees here come through postal service and they come *slooowww* ). 

I was mostly happy because it was remote back then with only one day in office as I was new. However, being in office alone with a few people was also quiet alright.

The company I joined was a small startup called *Onstro*, and what made it special was that it had quite an informal work culture which was 'the key' to my mental health.

Since then, I have progressed from a Trainee to Junior, then Software Engineer, and I am now serving as a **Senior Software Engineer**. My core expertise lies in the frontend ecosystem, specifically crafting robust interfaces with **JavaScript, React, Vue, Astro, Svelte and Next.js**.

My professional focus has been building enterprise-grade web applications that empower specialized users like researchers, scientists, and government officials to manage complex data securely and intuitively.

Being a startup it also let me use cool bleeding edge technology right of then bat.

All pretty cool.

## Onstro (formerly Teknorix)

As a full-time member of the team, I contribute to the architecture of our internal tools:

- [[Design Systems and Component Architecture]] : I focused on scaling a unified component library, building high-order components for complex data density and ensuring UX consistency across all client projects.
##  VIB (Vlaams Instituut voor Biotechnologie)

I worked on the frontend for the **[[VIB Research Ecosystem]]**, which is basically a giant suite of apps for the life sciences sector. It was a whole world of:

- **VIB Connect:** A portal for service discovery.
- **Research Tools:** Apps for data collection and publication indexing.
- **Public Interfaces:** The high-traffic side of things, like event ticketing and institutional outreach.

##  Ghana Goldbod
I even got to touch a [[Logistics and Supply Chain App]] for the secure transportation of gold. And yes, it used **blockchain**. My focus was on real-time data tracking and making sure the dashboards for logistics management actually made sense to the people using them.
