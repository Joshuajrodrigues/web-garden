[Vlaams Instituut voor Biotechnologie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vlaams_Instituut_voor_Biotechnologie) , VIB is a Belgium based research institute with a lot of data, students, scientific work and paperwork. 

All of this was needed to be managed securely, privately while at the same time provide user experience that's intuitive to users who might be not as tech savy, elderly or patient.


Vib publications
The scientists at VIB share their research through various scientific publications.
For this they have a Publications webapp.


Vib ticketing
Discover and book tickets for VIB events and activities through the official ticketing platform.


Vib connect 
To provide a seamless and unified access portal for all VIB service providers, we developed the VIB Connect. As technology is an essential cornerstone for the high-value research we do every day, we want to make sure that all VIB service providers are just a few clicks away.

The VIB Knowledge Base provides resources and information for research and collaboration in life sciences

Vib forms
VIB has created the VIB forms application to simplify data collection and compilation. This application is open for all VIB staff to start making your own surveys and automate your data collection processes, without any prior coding knowledge.

VIB forms:

has a user-friendly design
allows you to customize privacy settings
authenticity of all input data is verified
allows to create templates for re-use
is easily integrable with other VIB tools
 allows for gathering detailed statistics on the collected data

Vib grants
From the small stuff to the big picture - VIB grants empowers all stakeholders with guided workflows, tasks list and dashboard views to stay in sync and improve funding performance.
