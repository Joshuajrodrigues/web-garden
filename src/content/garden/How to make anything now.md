Do not find easy answers. 

Do not trust words you do not know. Ideas that aren't yours. 

Do not chase solutions to problems that don't exist, or sell your solution to people who don't have your problems.

Do not make it for money, make it because you can.

*When making.*

Do not confine yourself to your field. Consult art, music, movies, books they will all provide inspiration.

Enjoy not knowing and making mistakes, enjoy tumbling down rabbit hole of information, discovering ancient cryptic blogs of solutions to problems that aren't even remotely similar to yours. A breadcrumb trail to finding the right answer will form.

Answers that will be shot-down by your own self, sometimes rightfully so, as they will be wrong. But do not worry, you'll soon run out of being wrong.

Enjoy being the worst at what your doing, that moment wont last forever.

Enjoy being criticized and enjoy how all of this makes you better at what you do. Understand that not all of it is true, and not all of it is false. And know that you'll only be able to differentiate between the two after agonizing self criticism.

Remember failure doesn't exist. Neither does success.

Know that you will learn it, and it will click, and then you'll work for boring people who want boring things.

Know that sometimes work will be complicated, but not impossible. 

You'll find yourself asking, "Should it be built?" rather than "Can it be built.", ask this question more.

You'll see the things you want to make or are asked to make will challenge your values sometimes. Stay strong then. 

You'll find your ways to fight back, when you can, fight back by building something good. That will keep your soul.

In a false and do evil world, the most disruptive thing is to be good and true.
