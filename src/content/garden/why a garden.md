The internet should be a place of self expression and exploration with no borders on information or **walled gardens**.

Currently I feel like there is a [[war on information]]. And its majorly played out on the internet. A lot of the internet as a result is dead. It is now plagued by bots and trolls.

I believe the best way to win this war is to not participate. To throw them out. Because the internet is a place you visit. And you can choose to not visit some places.

I want to no longer contribute my time and energy for a dead space that we call the modern internet. 

This site serves as a document of everything I choose to share and record. This site also brings me joy and makes my senses calm down,

This place for you will be a mess, its intended to be. You do not have a menu or a nav-bar. You don't have any hand holding. Because you don't need it.

Wherever you land, whatever you find and however you feel about it is right. 

So I invite you to explore, there are tags that work as gentle identifiers on every post. These are liberally put.

The posts here range from unstructured rants to unfinished thoughts, options which end on question marks and a lot of experiments that are yet to be completed (Like this site).
